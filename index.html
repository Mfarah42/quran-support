The Madinah mushaf, page for page.

Quran is a free app with nothing in the way: no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions, and nothing collected about you. Read the printed 1421 H Madinah mushaf line for line, or a reading view with the translation beside every ayah. Listen to eleven reciters with each word lighting up as it is recited.

What it does

Accuracy first, then a clean page

  • The page as printed. Every page is set exactly as the 1421 H Madinah print, with the King Fahd Complex page fonts. Pinch to zoom, or make the text larger and let long lines wrap.
  • Reading view. The translation under each ayah, in the languages and order you choose. More than 120 translations in 69 languages, plus transliteration.
  • Word by word. The meaning and pronunciation under every word.
  • Recitation that follows along. Eleven reciters. The ayah and the word being recited light up; the page turns with the reciter; playback continues on the lock screen. Repeat an ayah, slow it down, set a sleep timer, download a surah to listen offline.
  • Never lose your place. The app remembers where you stopped. Mark ayat in four colours. Type a surah name, 2:255, page 77 or juz 5 to jump anywhere.
  • Free. Everything in the app. No ads, no accounts, no subscriptions.
FAQ

Frequently asked

Plain answers, the way the app gives them.

Where does the Quran text come from, and how do I know it is accurate?

The text is the Tanzil Uthmani text, checked against the mushaf's own page and glyph data from the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex, word by word, before the app is built. Nothing on the page is typed by hand: the words come from the layout, the surah headings from the metadata, the basmala from the text itself. If you ever see something that looks wrong, please email the surah and ayah number; it will be checked against the printed mushaf.

Which mushaf is this?

The 1421 H Madinah print (King Fahd Complex), 604 pages, 15 lines a page, Hafs ʿan ʿĀṣim. Page numbers, juz and hizb markers match that print.

Does it work offline?

The Quran text and the mushaf pages are inside the app and never need a connection. Translations, word meanings and recitations are downloaded when you choose them, and then work offline. Recitation streams by default; tap the speed chip in the player, then Download, to keep a surah on the device.

Why did a translation or the word meanings disappear, then come back?

Translations, word meanings and recitations come from the Quran Foundation, whose terms allow their content to be kept on a device for up to seven days. The app refreshes each downloaded file within that week. If a file could not be refreshed in time (for example, the phone was offline for more than a week), it is removed and downloaded again the next time you are online. Your place, your marks and your settings are never affected.

How do I change the text size?

Tap Aa in the reader. Arabic size and translation size each have a slider with a live preview. In the Mushaf view, 100% is the page as printed; larger sizes wrap long lines and the page scrolls. You can also pinch to zoom the page.

How do I mark an ayah or continue where I left off?

The app remembers where you stopped on its own; the Continue reading card on the home screen takes you back. To mark an ayah, tap it and choose one of four colours; marked ayat are washed in that colour on the page and listed under Marks. Continue from here on the same sheet sets your place by hand.

The highlight does not match the reciter.

Word timings are published by the Quran Foundation for each reciter and are checked against the page layout before they are used, but a few reciters' timings are looser than others. If the highlight is consistently off for one reciter, email the reciter and surah so it can be checked. On Bluetooth speakers and headphones the app already allows for the audio delay.

How do I reset the settings?

Settings → Restore default settings, at the bottom. Reading view, text sizes, translations, reciter and appearance go back to how the app came. Your place, marks and downloads are kept.

Does the app collect anything?

No. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only network use is downloading a translation, word meanings or a recitation you choose, and keeping those downloads up to date. See the privacy policy.
